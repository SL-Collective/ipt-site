Privacy policy — IPT

Last updated: 11 August 2026

The short version

IPT holds four things: your name, your email address, the practice you log, and the short audio clips you choose to record. They are used to run your studio and for nothing else.

There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party code of any kind in this app. Nothing is ever sold. Nothing is ever shared with anyone outside your studio.

That is the whole list. There is no location, no contacts, no photographs, no device identifier and no usage analytics.

Recordings

This is the most sensitive thing IPT holds, so it is worth being exact about.

  • The microphone is used only while you have tapped Record on the practice screen. Not in the background, not while the app is merely open, and never without the recording indicator showing.
  • A clip is stored privately. It is reachable only by the performer who made it and the instructors of the studio it was made in. This is enforced by the database, not by the app — a modified copy of the app could not reach somebody else's recording.
  • Clips are never public, never indexed, and have no shareable link.
  • No clip is ever used to train anything.

Face ID and your passcode

IPT can be set to ask for Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode when it is reopened. It is off unless you turn it on, in Settings → Privacy.

If you do turn it on, the app never sees your face, your fingerprint or your passcode. iOS asks on the app's behalf and answers yes or no; nothing biometric is stored by IPT, sent anywhere, or readable by it. The device passcode always works as well, so a lock can never leave you unable to reach your own practice.

Notifications

IPT sends reminders and, when your instructor listens to a take, a note saying so — which includes the sentence they wrote. That sentence can appear on your lock screen, where somebody else holding your phone could read it. If that matters to you, iOS Settings → Notifications → IPT → Show Previews controls it, and turning IPT's notifications off entirely is a single dial in Settings → Notifications inside the app.

Notifications are produced on your own device from data it already has. There is no push server, so nothing about you is sent anywhere in order to deliver one.

Who your data is shared with

Nobody, with two unavoidable exceptions:

  • Your studio. Your name, your section, and your practice totals are visible to the other people in your studio — that is what a leaderboard is. Your recordings, your notes and the notes your instructor writes back are not.
  • Our hosting provider (Supabase), which stores the database and the audio on our behalf and is not permitted to use it for anything else.

Nothing is sold, and nothing is passed to an advertiser or a data broker, because there are none.

Students under 13, and schools

IPT is designed to be used by a school or an ensemble, and the audio it collects is personal information under the United States' Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) when the performer is under 13.

Where IPT is used in a school, the school may consent on behalf of parents, but only where the information is collected for the school's own educational purpose and for no commercial purpose. That is exactly how IPT is used: there is no advertising here and nothing is sold, so there is no other purpose for it to be used for.

If you run a programme with performers under 13, you should follow your district's process for approving a classroom tool before you invite them. If your district needs a data agreement in place, ask and one will be provided.

There is no profile photograph feature in IPT, deliberately, and there is no messaging between performers.

Deleting things

  • Leave a studio — Settings → Leaving. Your membership goes. The practice you already logged stays part of that studio's record, the same way it would in a paper gradebook; deleting your account is what removes it.
  • Delete your account — Settings → Leaving → Delete my account. This removes your profile, all of your practice, all of your recordings, and any studio you own along with everything logged in it. The app tells you exactly how much that is before it does anything. It cannot be undone.

There is no waiting period and no email to send. Deletion happens immediately, from inside the app.

How long things are kept

Until you delete them. IPT does not expire recordings or practice history to save storage — a performer's record of their own work is the product, and quietly deleting it to reduce a bill would be the wrong trade.

Security

  • Everything travels over HTTPS.
  • Access is enforced by row-level security in the database, so what the app is allowed to read is the same as what the product permits — not a filter the app applies to itself.
  • Audio lives in a private store reached only through short-lived links generated per playback. There is no durable public address for a recording.

Your rights

You can see everything IPT holds about you from inside the app, correct your name and instrument in Settings, and delete all of it at any time. If you are in the UK or EU and want a copy of your data in a portable form, or the GDPR rights that go beyond the above, contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who can see it, the app will say so before the change takes effect rather than only updating this page.
