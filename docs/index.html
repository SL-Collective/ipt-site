Assign the work, not the minutes

A time target says how long, not what to work on. Give them the instruction you would give in the room: hands separate, take it at 72, fix the release in bar 12. They have it on Tuesday night when you are not there.

Hear how it went, not just how long

Performers record a short take with their own click playing out loud, so you can hear how the playing and the click line up. They mark the moments worth hearing, which turns a four-minute take into a ten-second listen.

Thirty seconds on a Monday

Your dashboard puts whoever needs you first, and folds everybody who is doing fine out of the way. Listen to a take and send one line back. That is what keeps them recording.
