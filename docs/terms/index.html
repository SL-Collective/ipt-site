What an account is

An IPT account costs $4.99 once, for one person, bought on the web at iptmusic.com. Bought inside the iPhone app it costs the same price in your own currency, set by Apple for the country your App Store account is in. Either way it does not renew and it is never billed again. With it you can create studios, join studios with a code an instructor gives you, log practice, record short audio clips, and read whatever your own studio shows you.

An App Store purchase is a purchase from Apple, and Apple's terms apply to the payment itself, including refunds. Refunds says exactly who does what.

You can use one account on every device you sign in on. It is yours, not a device's.

Trying IPT needs no account at all: the demo studio is the whole app with seeded data and it is free and unlimited.

Who may use it

IPT is built for school and studio music programs, which means many of the people using it are minors.

  • If you are 13 or older, you may create an account yourself.
  • If you are under 13, an account may only be created for you by a school or program acting under its own parental-consent process, or by a parent or guardian directly. IPT relies on the school-consent route described in the privacy policy, and a program using IPT is confirming it has that consent.

An instructor who invites performers into a studio is responsible for having the standing to do so within their program.

What you agree to do, and not do

  • Do not upload a recording of somebody who has not agreed to be recorded. This is the one that matters most, and it is the one a school will ask about. Clips are heard by your instructor and by nobody else outside your studio.
  • Do not use IPT to harass anybody. Instructors can write a line back on a recording and can send a performer a note; both are for teaching.
  • Do not try to reach data belonging to a studio you are not in, or to grant yourself an account you have not paid for. The server refuses both, and attempting it is a reason to close an account.
  • Do not resell IPT accounts or share one account between several people.
